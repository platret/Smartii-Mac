Why Smartii for Mac

An assistant that lives in the menu bar.

Not a window. Not a tab. A keystroke that reaches across every app on your screen.

Menu bar, not a window

Runs as an accessory app — no Dock icon, no clutter. A ✦ in your menu bar is the whole UI. Answers appear in a floating panel that vanishes when you're done.

⚡ Godmode

One press → Smartii reads everything on screen and answers it all, no typing. Quizzes, code, errors, multiple-choice — numbered, with the reasoning and the fix.

Sees your whole screen

Captures your display with ScreenCaptureKit and sends it to a vision model. Errors, diagrams, exams, any app — not just the browser.

Your key, your data

Bring your own API key. Stored in the macOS Keychain, sent straight to the provider you chose. No Smartii server, no telemetry, no middleman.

7 providers in one

Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, Hugging Face, Anthropic, OpenAI, Perplexity. Switch from the menu. Vision routing picks the right model automatically.

— what it sees Not just a browser tab. The entire desktop — every app, every window, the whole screen.

⚡ Godmode

It does it all for you.

Hit ⌥⇧G. Smartii captures your entire screen, finds every question, problem, error or task on it, and answers them all — directly and completely.

  • Multiple-choice: the correct letter and the reasoning
  • Code or error: the actual fix, ready to paste
  • Multiple questions: every answer, numbered in order
  • Whatever's on screen — Finder, an IDE, a PDF, a quiz

Setup

Install in under a minute.

No App Store, no sign-up. Download, drag, open.

  1. 1
    Download Smartii.dmg from GitHub Releases and open it.
  2. 2
    Drag Smartii into the Applications folder shown in the window.
  3. 3
    Right-click → Open the first time. The app is unsigned, so macOS asks you to confirm — click Open.
  4. 4
    Grant Screen Recording when prompted, then pick a provider and paste your API key in Settings.
